# Putting Valley Life on itch.io

Everything you need is in this folder and on the
[Releases page](https://github.com/davidvstuhl-prog/Valley-Life/releases/latest):

| File | Where it goes on itch.io |
|---|---|
| `ValleyLife-web.zip` (from Releases) | Uploads, as the browser version |
| `ValleyLife-Windows.zip` (from Releases) | Uploads, as the Windows download |
| `cover-630x500.png` | Cover image |
| `screenshot-1` to `screenshot-6` | Screenshots |

Ask a parent or guardian to help with the account. itch.io has a minimum age for accounts,
so check their rules when you sign up.

---

## Step by step

1. Sign in at https://itch.io, open the menu next to your name, and click **Upload new project**.
2. **Title:** `Valley Life`
3. **Project URL:** `valley-life`
4. **Short description or tagline:** copy the tagline below.
5. **Classification:** Games
6. **Kind of project:** HTML (the browser version is the main one; the Windows file is an extra download)
7. **Release status:** In development
8. **Pricing:** No payments (free). You can choose "Donations" instead if you want tips.
9. **Uploads:**
   - Upload `ValleyLife-web.zip` and tick **This file will be played in the browser**.
   - Upload `ValleyLife-Windows.zip` and tick **Windows** next to it.
10. **Embed options** (appear after the browser upload):
    - Viewport dimensions: **1280 × 760**
    - Tick **Mobile friendly** and pick **Landscape**
    - Tick **Fullscreen button**
11. **Description:** copy everything in the "Description" section below.
12. **Genre:** Simulation
13. **Tags:** fishing, farming, crafting, pets, survival, cozy, exploration, 2d, top-down, singleplayer
14. **AI generation disclosure:** this game's code and art were made with help from an AI (Claude),
    so answer **Yes** and say so honestly.
15. **Cover image:** upload `cover-630x500.png`.
16. **Screenshots:** upload all six screenshots.
17. **Visibility:** leave it on **Draft** and click **Save**. Then click **View page**, play it there to
    make sure it works, and when you're happy, change Visibility to **Public** and save again.

When you change the game later, download the new zips from Releases and replace the old
uploads on your itch.io project (Edit game, then Uploads).

## Description

**Valley Life** is a cozy do-everything adventure set in a hand-drawn valley full of forests,
rivers, caves and a little town. Catch fish, grow crops, dig up treasure, craft your own gear,
run a restaurant and hatch adorable pets, all while keeping your health, food, water and oxygen up.

**What you can do**

- **Fish:** cast into rivers, lakes and a hidden cave pool, then reel in 24 kinds of fish, from
  Minnows to the legendary Golden Carp and the mythic Lake Serpent. Some only bite at night.
- **Chop:** six named forests, each with its own trees. Time your swing for a PERFECT hit, and
  watch out for rare glowing **Ancient Trees**.
- **Mine & dig:** break ore in the cave (a weak pickaxe still works, just slowly), dig up fossils,
  crowns and alien eggs, or dive for pearls and sunken chests.
- **Farm:** plant nine crops that grow in real time. Watch for Golden and Rainbow mutations.
- **Hunt:** rabbits, deer, boars, wolves at night, bears, and the rare Golden Stag.
- **Cook & run a restaurant:** turn your catch into Fish Tacos and Hunter's Feasts, then serve
  diners for tips.
- **Craft:** 33 recipes at the Workshop: planks, rope, metal bars, tools, backpacks, and charms that
  give permanent bonuses.
- **Pets:** hatch 18 pets in 6 rarities. They follow you around and give bonuses.
- **Townsfolk:** wandering buyers explore the valley looking for things you can sell them.
- Upgrade tree, quests, day and night, rain, and a Boost Shop.

No real-money purchases. Everything is bought with coins you earn by playing.

**Controls**

| Keyboard + mouse | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD / arrows | Walk (Shift to sprint) |
| Left click / E | Chop, mine, axe animals up close, use things |
| Right click | Throw spear |
| Hold F, aim, let go | Cast your fishing rod |
| Hold Space or mouse | Reel in |
| Hold Q | Dig (or dive in deep water) |
| R | Eat your best food |
| Tab / C / K / P / G | Backpack / crafting / upgrades / pets / boosts |

On a touchscreen? Pick **Touchscreen** when the game asks, and you get a joystick and big buttons.

Your progress saves automatically in your browser (or next to the game on Windows).

**Windows version:** download the zip, unzip it, and double-click `ValleyLife.exe`. If Windows says
"Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
